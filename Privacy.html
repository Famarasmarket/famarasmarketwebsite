Privacy Policy

How Famara'smarket collects, uses and protects customer information.

Last Updated:

Famara'smarket respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we may collect when you use our website, create an account, contact us, place an order, submit a review or use other services provided through our website.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use Famara'smarket, we may collect information such as:

  • Full name
  • Phone number
  • Email address
  • Delivery area and delivery address
  • Customer account information
  • Order information
  • Payment method and payment reference supplied by the customer
  • Customer reviews and ratings
  • Messages and information submitted through contact forms
  • Newsletter subscription information

2. How We Use Your Information

Information may be used to:

  • Create and manage customer accounts
  • Process and manage orders
  • Arrange delivery
  • Contact customers about their orders
  • Confirm or discuss payments
  • Respond to customer questions
  • Provide customer support
  • Improve our products and services
  • Manage customer reviews
  • Send newsletters or business updates when customers subscribe
  • Protect the website against misuse and fraud

3. Customer Accounts

If you create an account, information associated with your account may be stored so that you can access account-related features.

You should keep your password and login information confidential and notify us if you believe someone has accessed your account without permission.

4. Orders and Delivery Information

When you place an order, we need certain information to process the order and arrange delivery.

This may include:

  • Your name
  • Phone number
  • Delivery area
  • Delivery address
  • Order items
  • Payment information supplied by you
  • Additional delivery instructions

5. Payment Information

Famara'smarket may offer payment options such as Orange Money, Afrimoney, bank payments and Cash on Delivery.

When a customer provides a payment reference or transaction number, it may be used to help identify and verify the customer's payment.

Customers should avoid sending passwords, PINs, security codes or other confidential banking credentials through the website or WhatsApp.

6. Customer Reviews

If you submit a review or rating, the information you provide may be displayed on the website according to our review system.

Do not include sensitive personal information, passwords, banking credentials or other private information in a public review.

7. Contact Forms and Messages

If you contact Famara'smarket, we may keep the information necessary to respond to your message and provide customer support.

8. Newsletter

If you subscribe to the Famara'smarket newsletter, your email address may be used to send business updates, announcements, promotions or other information related to Famara'smarket.

You may request to stop receiving marketing communications.

9. Cookies and Local Storage

The website may use browser storage technologies such as local storage to support website features.

These features may be used to help remember:

  • Shopping cart information
  • Certain website preferences
  • Order information during the current website setup
  • Other functionality required by the website

10. Sharing Information

Famara'smarket does not intend to sell customer personal information.

Information may be shared when reasonably necessary to operate the business, process an order, arrange delivery, provide customer support, comply with legal obligations or protect the website and its users.

11. WhatsApp Communication

Famara'smarket may use WhatsApp to communicate with customers about orders and customer service.

When you choose to contact or place an order through WhatsApp, your communication is also subject to WhatsApp's own terms and privacy practices.

12. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information handled through the website.

However, no website or electronic transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

13. Data Retention

Information may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary for order processing, customer support, business records, legal obligations, dispute resolution and other legitimate business purposes.

14. Children's Privacy

Famara'smarket is intended for customers who are legally able to use the services and make purchases.

We do not knowingly request unnecessary personal information from children.

15. Third-Party Services

The website may use third-party services such as payment providers, communication services, analytics tools, hosting services or social-media platforms.

Those services may process information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

16. Your Privacy Questions

If you have questions about information associated with your account, order or communication with Famara'smarket, please contact us.

Visit our Contact Us page for available contact options.

17. Changes to This Privacy Policy

Famara'smarket may update this Privacy Policy from time to time as the website and business develop.

When changes are made, the updated policy will be published on this page with a new update date.

Important: This Privacy Policy is written for the current Famara'smarket website features. Before the public launch, we should review it against the final technical setup, actual data practices, third-party services and applicable Sierra Leone privacy requirements.
